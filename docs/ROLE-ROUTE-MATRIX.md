# Role × route matrix (permission sweep, 2026-11-18; updated for SR-1 / 9.2 / 9.3, countries, the primary teacher and Test Management Phases 1 and 2, owner decisions 2026-11-19)

Generated by `src/auth/role-route-sweep.spec.ts` (backend `feat/teacher-student-access`) with `WRITE_ROLE_MATRIX`: the real
guard chain (JwtAuthGuard → RolesGuard → PermissionGuard) decides each of the 369 routes the application registers, for
every caller. ✓ = passes the guards; the service then applies record-level rules (own records, course × division,
programme or school scope, 404 for what may not be seen).

Invariants the test enforces: anonymous only on @Public routes; no Student or Teacher on an /admin route; /teacher routes
only for Teachers (Head Coaches and Administrators pass the guard for the list, the summary and the teacher's own schools and are refused by the service
with 403 ROLE_NOT_SUPPORTED); /mentor routes closed to Students and Teachers; Head Coaches reach exactly 42 /admin routes
(their programme management and Test Management in the subjects they head, programme-scoped in the services); Super Administrator ⊇ Administrator (+6: permission
overrides, school-directory sync approve / apply / resolve); no administrator role on a grading route; per-role totals
frozen (anonymous 26, Student 102, Teacher 92, Coach 110, Head Coach 146, Administrator 316, Super Administrator 322; countries added the public list and 2 reference.manage routes for Administrators and Super Admins; the primary teacher added the reassignment route for Administrators and Super Admins and GET /teacher/schools for Teachers; Test Management Phase 1 added 19 /admin/tests and /admin/test-markers routes for Head Coaches, Administrators and Super Admins; Test Management Phase 2 added 8 /me/tests routes for Students, 9 /teacher/tests routes for Teachers and 8 /admin/tests/:id extension and submission routes for Head Coaches, Administrators and Super Admins).

After the guards above, DateOfBirthGuard (decision 9.3) holds a Student without a date of birth on GET /me, GET
/me/permissions, PUT /me/date-of-birth and sign-out until they give it; the matrix shows role access only. SR-1: the
/teacher student routes return only ACTIVE student accounts (service rule, not a guard).
